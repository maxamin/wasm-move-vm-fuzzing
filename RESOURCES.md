- [wasm-tools](https://github.com/bytecodealliance/wasm-tools) (wasm-smith, wasm-mutate, shrink, validate, print)
- [Wasmtime](https://github.com/bytecodealliance/wasmtime) and its [fuzzing docs](https://docs.wasmtime.dev/contributing-fuzzing.html), [wasmi](https://github.com/wasmi-labs/wasmi)
- [WebAssembly spec](https://webassembly.github.io/spec/) and [proposals repository](https://github.com/WebAssembly/proposals)
- [cargo-fuzz](https://github.com/rust-fuzz/cargo-fuzz) and the [Rust Fuzz Book](https://rust-fuzz.github.io/book/)
- [LibAFL](https://github.com/AFLplusplus/LibAFL)
- [OSS-Fuzz](https://github.com/google/oss-fuzz)
